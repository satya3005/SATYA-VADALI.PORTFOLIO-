## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, high-contrast dark visual identity for Vadali Satya Sai Sandeep (Satya Vadali), a Graphic Designer and Digital Marketing Associate specializing in GenAI and brand storytelling. The aesthetic fuses technical precision with creative luminescence—evoking generative intelligence, forward-leaning media strategy, and refined design craft.

### Visual Aesthetic & Style Direction
- **Style Blend:** Modern Cyber-Minimalism layered over Glassmorphism and Luminescent Accents. 
- **Atmosphere:** Deep void obsidian backdrop punctuated by laser-sharp neon cyan impulses and warm kinetic amber glows. It creates the sensation of peering into a next-generation neural interface curated by a meticulous art director.
- **Emotional Response:** Innovative, technically masterly, high-caliber, and magnetic.
- **Key Motifs:** Translucent frosted surfaces, fine 1px light-catching boundary lines, radial gradient underglows, and stark typographical scale contrast.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an abyss-level charcoal black, punctuated by sharp bioluminescent lights and radiant solar flares to delineate GenAI logic from human creative storytelling.

### Functional Palette
- **Deep Obsidian Ground (`#0a0b10`):** The foundational canvas. Provides maximum dynamic range and negative space for imagery and work showcases.
- **Sub-Surface Tier (`#12141c`):** Utilized for structural layers, cards, and docked panels.
- **Neon Cyan Primary (`#00f2fe`):** Applied to primary actions, active navigation states, interactive links, metrics highlights, and critical UI nodes. Evokes pure intelligence and digital agility.
- **Electric Amber Secondary (`#ff9900`):** Used for storytelling markers, awards, key badges, creative impact callouts, and secondary visual balances.
- **Solar Glow Tertiary (`#ffb347`):** Soft luminous highlights, hover glows, and ambient gradient stops.
- **Boundary & Surface Stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): High-precision hairline borders delineating cards, inputs, and modular layouts.
- **Text Tiers:**
  - Headings & Primary Text: `#f4f6fa` (crisp white with minute cool tint)
  - Secondary Text: `#9ba1b0` (mid-tone desaturated slate)
  - Dim / Tertiary Text: `#5d6373` (subtle framing details, timestamps)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the architectural, geometric flair of **Space Grotesk** with the refined, fluid legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Provides technological character and commanding modern energy. Headings leverage tightened letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to maintain optical density and authority in both hero headers and project titles.
- **Body & Longform Copy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances technical geometry with organic, friendly curves that ensure prolonged readability across campaign breakdowns, case studies, and editorial write-ups.
- **Labels, Badges, and Micro-Data (Space Grotesk Uppercase):** Rendered with intentional positive tracking (`+0.06em` to `+0.1em`) to mimic telemetry data, AI model tags, and technical classifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an uncompromising 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous structural breathing room, designed to present media-rich assets and high-fidelity portfolios without clutter.

### Layout Philosophy & Grid
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid container with a max width of `1360px`, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `3rem` (48px) margins. Elements span 3, 4, 6, 8, or 12 units to maintain geometric asymmetry.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standardized `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Consolidated layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins, collapsing multi-column portfolio grids into singular cinematic vertical flows.
- **Spacing Principle:** Consistent multiples of 8px (supplemented with 4px intervals for tight chip and button groupings). Generous vertical padding (`space-2xl` between sections) ensures the dark aesthetic feels cavernous, curated, and premium.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered frosted glass, dark structural fills, and colored radial backlights rather than traditional mud-gray drop shadows.

### Glassmorphism & Translucency Rules
- **Base Canvas:** Solid `#0a0b10`.
- **Card & Sheet Surfaces:** Background `rgba(18, 20, 28, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and `-webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.
- **Outer Edge Boundaries:** Strict `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to deliver clean, crisp silhouettes. Hover states step this boundary up to `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.35)` or `rgba(255, 153, 0, 0.35)`.
- **Luminescent Glows & Shadows:**
  - *Cyan Ambient Glow:* `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.3)`. Applied to active triggers and prominent interactive cards upon hover.
  - *Amber Ambient Glow:* `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 153, 0, 0.3)`. Reserved for featured creative campaign highlights and spotlight badges.
  - *Subtle Card Depth:* `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a refined modern rounded aesthetic (`Level 2`).

- **Default Element Radius:** `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, inputs, tags, and small utility elements.
- **Cards & Modal Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) rounding, giving glass surfaces smooth structural containment.
- **Hero Banners & Feature Highlights (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) for prominent media showcases and floating dynamic docks.
- **Badges & Status Pills:** Full circular caps (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to cleanly offset rectangular card frameworks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Glowing Button:**
  - Background: Solid `#00f2fe` with text in `#0a0b10` (weight: 600).
  - Hover: Subtle scale transition with an intense bloom glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 28px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Glass Button:**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
  - Text: `#f4f6fa`.
  - Hover: Border switches to `rgba(255, 153, 0, 0.5)`, text becomes `#ff9900`.
- **Tertiary Minimal Button:**
  - Borderless, plain text link styled with `Space Grotesk` uppercase tracking, transitioning to `#00f2fe` on interaction with an animated underline rule.

### Glowing Badges & Chips
- **GenAI / Capability Badges:**
  - Height: Compact padding (`space-xs` vertical, `space-sm` horizontal).
  - Background: `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.08)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.25)`.
  - Font: `label-sm`, all caps, colored `#00f2fe`. Includes a 6px pulsing dot.
- **Creative / Storytelling Badges:**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 153, 0, 0.08)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 153, 0, 0.25)`.
  - Font: `label-sm`, all caps, colored `#ff9900`.

### Glass Portfolio Cards
- Background: Translucent obsidian `#12141c` at 65% opacity with 16px backdrop blur.
- Outer Frame: Continuous hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Internal Layout: Media frame on top, spaced with `space-md` gaps down to project title, metadata pills, and campaign summary.
- Hover Effect: The top edge captures an animated linear gradient highlight running from `#00f2fe` to `#ff9900`, paired with an ambient backlight glow.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Focus State: Edge transitions to `#00f2fe` accompanied by `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.25)`.
- Typography: `body-md` in `#f4f6fa`, with placeholder copy rendered in `#5d6373`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Base: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, background: `rgba(18, 20, 28, 0.8)`.
- Active / Checked: Fill shifts to `#00f2fe` with an embedded obsidian checkmark icon and miniature cyan halo glow.

### Portfolio-Specific Components
- **AI Prompt & Process Terminal:** A specialized code/prompt preview box featuring a monospaced styling pass, dark glass styling, and an amber copy-to-clipboard trigger.
- **Interactive Metric Callouts:** Giant numeral displays in `Space Grotesk` Display sizing with cyan gradient masking to highlight performance metrics, CTR gains, and GenAI production efficiencies.
